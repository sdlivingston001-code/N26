Shooting Sequence

Resolve a ranged attack step by step: Declare the Shot → Measure Range → Make the Hit Roll → Resolve Hits.

1. Declare the Shot

Choose a ranged weapon profile the attacking model is equipped with.

2. Measure Range

Choose whether the target is at Short or Long range for this weapon.

3. Make the Hit Roll

A natural 1 always misses; a natural 6 always hits — regardless of modifiers.

(e.g. 4 for BS 4+)
(0 = single shot, no Firepower dice)

4. Resolve Hits

A. Make a Wound Roll

B. Make a Save Roll

C. Inflict Damage

Nerve Check

If a model is within 3" of a friendly model that goes Out of Action, they must make a Cool check or become Suppressed.

(e.g. 7 for Cool 7+)
Arbitrator's Toolkit
  • Stray Shots: If the attack misses, roll a D6 for each model within 1" of the line along which range was measured, starting with the model closest to the attacker. On a 1, 2, or 3 that model is Hit; on a 4, 5, or 6 the shot misses them and you move to the next model at risk.
  • Target Priority: If the closest model in Line of Sight is within 12" and is not Engaged or Seriously Injured/Damaged, they must be the target of any ranged attack unless the attacking model passes a Cool check.
  • Walking Fire: If a Rapid Fire weapon scores more than one Hit, the Hits can be split between multiple targets. The first must be allocated to the original target; the remainder can go to other enemy models within 3" of the first who are also within range and Line of Sight. Allocate all Hits before making any Wound rolls.
