The Post Battle Sequence

House rules remove Into Recovery and Convalescence to simplify admin.

The rules below reflect the campaign House rules.

**Immediately after the game**

1. Wrap-Up

A. Succumbing to Injuries

If a fighter was:

  • Prone & Seriously Injured when the Battle ended, or
  • Prone & Seriously Injured when they fled the battle (e.g. after a gang had bottled out)

Roll 1D6, on a 1 or 2 they suffer a Lasting Injury.


B. Did Captured fighters escape?

For each friendly fighter that suffered a lasting injury of CAPTURED, test to see if they escape.


C. Ransoming Captured fighters

Fighters are always released back to their gangs after the battle. The question is whether they will have all their body parts...

  • Enhanced Guilder Bounties! A leader or champion of the Bountied Gang may be ransomed back for 50% of their credit value
  • Standard Bounties: Any other fighters can be ransomed back for 25% of their credit value.
  • Prisoner Exchange: If both gangs agree, other fighters can be exchanged on a fighter-for-fighter basis without either gang paying a ransom.

Fighters that weren't exchanged or ransomed suffer a Lasting Injury!


D. Claim Guilder Bounties

If fighting the "Bountied Gang" then you can earn additional credits from ONE of the following options:

  • 100 credits if at least one Leader or Champion was killed (includes if they die during treatment for Critical Injuries).
  • 50 credits if at least one Leader or Champion went Out of Action.

The 100 / 50 credits are paid by local guilders (not your opponent).


2. Receive Scenario Rewards

Follow the scenario rules as normal.


3. Assign / Reassign Territories

The Arbitrator will give guidance.


4. Collect Territory Income/Boons

The Arbitrator will give guidance.

If generating income, the campaign uses a simplified set of territory rules: Income & Boons


E. Clean House (Memorable Deaths)

  • Delete dead fighters from the roster.
  • Add their equipment (except armour) to the gang stash if and only if at the end of the battle there was at least one non-dead friendly fighter still present on the battlefield

**At Players' Leisure**

Post Battle Actions

The campaign does not require individual champions/leaders to perform each action.

The following actions have been removed: Sell to Guilders, Claim Bounties, Dispose of Captives

The following actions can be taken in any order:

  • F. Medical Escort
  • G. Visit the Trading Post
  • H. Gang specific actions

F. Treat Critical Injuries

Any fighter can drag any critically injured fighter to a Rogue Doc for treatment (i.e. this action does not require a fighter with the Gang Hierarchy(X) rule).


G. Visit the Trading Post

If you resolve the Medical Escort actions first, then all fighters with the relevant rules can contribute to this check.


Rare & Illegal level: This single roll represents the best effort of the gang over the days/weeks between fights to source rare and illegal equipment.

Common items are always available. There are no Caryatid generated by this roll.


These additional actions are available now:

  • Hire a Fighter from your gang list
  • Any non-Brute gang fighter can purchase & equip equipment from it's own Equipment list
  • Hire a Hanger-on (spend credits + 3 Rep)
  • Hire a Brute (spend credits + 5 Rep, equipment cannot be upgraded later)
  • Sell unwanted equipment from the gang stash (Price less D6*10 credits)

H. Gang / Alliance / Territory / Hanger-on... Post-Battle Actions

Examples of gang actions:

  • Join a cult
  • Install / repair Archaeotech-Cybernetika
  • Chem-Alchemy
  • ...

Remember that Into Recovery and Convalescence have been removed in this campaign.


Update Roster

As normal, in this order:

  • I. Clean House (Died from Critical Injury)
  • J. Distribute Equipment
  • K. Purchase Advancements

I. Clean House (Died from Critical Injury)

  • Delete dead fighters from the roster.
  • Add their equipment (except armour) to the gang stash

J. Distribute Equipment

Any items in the gang stash can be distributed among the gang members.

Be careful distributing weapons as not all fighter profiles allow all weapons (either restricted by class / source). For example:

  • Heavy weapons are typically restricted to leaders / champions
  • Special weapons are typically restricted to leaders / champions / specialists
  • Gangers are typically allowed Basic, Pistol, and Close Combat weapons from the Trading Post
  • Prospects are typically restricted to their own equipment list plus some classes of Trading Post weapons
  • Juves are typically restricted to non-special non-heavy weapons from their own equipment list (i.e. not from the Trading Post)

Typically weapons are not allowed to be removed from fighters once allocated - if you are providing a serious upgrade the arbitrator may make an exception.

If you have a Territory that provides equipment add it at this step (Change the cost to 0 and untick 'Use Listed Cost for Rating')


K. Purchase Advancements
