Model Types and Sub-Types

All models are either Fighters or Vehicles.

Fighter

Models with the Fighter Type are the backbone of gangs, their footsoldiers and leaders. The majority of models in a Necromunda gang will be Fighters.

Vehicle

Vehicles are far more restricted than Fighters with regard to the actions they can perform during a battle and the Post-cycle Sequence; however, they give gangs access to powerful weaponry upon a fast chassis. Vehicles and their crew are inseparable and when the rules refer to Vehicles, they can refer to either the Vehicle itself or its crew.

When a Vehicle is removed from the Gang Roster, all weapons and wargear it is equipped with are deleted instead of going into the gang's Stash.

All Vehicles have the Juggernaut skill.

Status

A model can only have one status at a time. Where a model qualifies for more than one, the lower status in this list takes priority.

Nerve Check: if a friendly model (not a pet) goes Out of Action within 3", pass a Cool check or become Suppressed. (Prospects only cause this check for other Prospects.)

Conditions

Minor, persistent effects a model can suffer in addition to its status.
