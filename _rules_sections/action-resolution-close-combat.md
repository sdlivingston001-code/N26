#### Engaged Actions

| Action | Type | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Fight | Double | Resolve close combat. |
| Retreat | Double | Move up to Initiative" out of engagement. |

#### Close Combat Sequence

1. **Determine fighters** involved.
2. **Choose weapons** — the activating model chooses Primary and Secondary weapons from those with the Melee or Light traits; other participants choose only a Primary weapon. Light weapons make a maximum of 1 attack.
3. **Determine attack dice** — activating model: Primary weapon makes A attacks (+1 if charged), Secondary weapon makes 1 attack; other participants: Primary weapon (Melee or Light) makes 1 attack only.
4. **Declare targets.**
5. **Determine initiative order** — Unwieldy sets Initiative to 1; +2 Initiative if charging. Fighters with equal Initiative strike simultaneously.
6. **Roll to Hit** (≥ WS of the fighter). -1 if not in line of sight before the charge/engage; +1 for Claiming Assistance (another friend is also engaged with the only enemy target); -1 for Suffering Interference (a second enemy is also engaged with the attacker and no other friends are). A natural 6 always hits, a natural 1 always fails.
7. **Resolve hits** — see **Resolve Hits**.
8. **Consolidate** — if all engaged enemies are Out of Action or Seriously Injured, may move up to Initiative" but cannot end within 1" of an enemy.
