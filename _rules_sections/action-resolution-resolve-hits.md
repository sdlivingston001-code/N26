1. **Make a wound roll (D6)**

   | Strength vs Toughness | Roll needed |
   |---|---|
   | S double T | 2+ |
   | S &gt; T | 3+ |
   | S = T | 4+ |
   | S &lt; T | 5+ |
   | S half T | 6+ |

2. **Make a save roll** (≥ Sv of the target). A natural 1 or 2 always fails. Shooting modifiers: +1 if the target is in cover at short range; +2 if in cover at long range.
3. **Inflict damage** — the target loses wounds; if it reaches 0, the opponent rolls a number of Injury dice equal to the weapon's Lethality and chooses one result to apply.

Possible outcomes: **Injured/Damaged** (condition), **Seriously Injured/Damaged** (status), or **Out of Action**.
