---
title: Recovery Tests
parent: action-resolution
order: 7
---

A Seriously Injured/Damaged model makes a Recovery test (rolling Injury dice) after activating.

- Only an **Injured/Damaged** result allows the model to recover from Seriously Injured/Damaged; any other result leaves it Seriously Injured/Damaged, or takes it Out of Action.
- If the test is made within 1" of an enemy who is not also Seriously Injured/Damaged, the model automatically goes Out of Action instead.
- **Assistance:** roll an extra Injury die if a friendly model is within 1" and is not Engaged or Seriously Injured/Damaged, then choose 1 of the results.
