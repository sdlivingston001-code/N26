---
title: Nerve Tests
slug: nerve-tests
group: resolve
order: 11
---

If a friendly model (not a pet) goes Out of Action within 3", pass a **Cool** check or become Suppressed.

- Prospects only cause this check for other Prospects.
- Friendly models within 12" and in Line of Sight of a Leader, or within 6" and in Line of Sight of a Champion, may use that model's Cool characteristic for the check.
