Models with the **Wyrd** subtype can Manifest and Disrupt psychic powers.

| Action | Type | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Manifest Wyrd Power | Varies by power | The activating Wyrd makes a Manifesting Willpower check; an opposing Wyrd may make a Disrupting Willpower check. A Disrupting check passed by a non-Injured Wyrd (Active or Suppressed) within 18" and line of sight gives a -2 modifier to the Manifesting check — only one disruption attempt is allowed per power. For both checks, a natural 2 or 12 causes Perils of the Warp, and additionally: Manifester rolls 2 = success; Manifester rolls 12 = fail; Disruptor rolls 2 = Manifest fails; Disruptor rolls 12 = no disruption. |
| Maintain Control | Free or Single | Willpower check. If passed, the power remains until the end of the model's next activation; if failed, it ends immediately. The Single-action version gives +3 to the check; the Free version is made at the start of the activation with no modifier. |
| Concentrate | Single | +1 modifier to the Manifesting Willpower check made this activation. |
