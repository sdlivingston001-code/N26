#### Shooting Actions

| Action | Type | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Shoot | Single | Make an attack with a ranged weapon (not one with the Heavy trait). |
| Aimed Shot | Double | As Shoot, with +1 to hit (not for weapons with the Heavy trait). |
| Braced Shot | Double | Shoot with a weapon that has the Heavy trait. |
| Fire All | Double | Fire all of the model's weapons, including Heavy ones, each at a target declared before any rolls are made. |
| Reload | Single | Reload a ranged weapon that is Out of Ammo. |

#### Shooting Sequence

1. **Declare target** in line of sight.
2. **Measure range.**
3. **Roll to Hit** (≥ BS of the shooter). -1 modifier against a Seriously Injured/Damaged target; random target allocation when firing at an Engaged model. A natural 6 always hits, a natural 1 always fails.
4. **Suppress the target** if hit (and make an Agility test or fall, if within 0.5" of a ledge).
5. **Resolve the hit** — see **Resolve Hits**.
