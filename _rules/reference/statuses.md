---
title: Statuses
order: 3
---

A model can only have one status at a time. Where a model qualifies for more than one, the lower status in this list takes priority.
{: .text-muted .text-small}

| Status | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Active** | Ready to activate with 2 actions. No restrictions. |
| **Suppressed** | Only has 1 action when activated. |
| **Engaged** | Base contact (Fighters) / within 1" (Vehicles) of an enemy. Limited actions: Fight or Retreat. Seriously Injured/Damaged models cannot Engage or be Engaged. Engaging is optional at the start of an activation. |
| **Seriously Injured** (Fighters) / **Seriously Damaged** (Vehicles) | Limited actions; must make a Recovery test after activating. |
| **Out of Action (OOA)** | Roll on the D66 Lasting Injury (Fighters) or Lasting Damage (Vehicles) table. |

See also [Nerve Tests](#nerve-tests).
