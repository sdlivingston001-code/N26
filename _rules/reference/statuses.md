---

A model can only have one status at a time. Where a model qualifies for more than one, the lower status in this list takes priority.
{: .text-muted .text-small}

| Status | Effect |
|---|---|
| <span id="statuses-active">**Active**</span> | Ready to activate with 2 actions. No restrictions. |
| <span id="statuses-suppressed">**Suppressed**</span> | Only has 1 action when activated. |
| <span id="statuses-engaged">**Engaged**</span> | Base contact (Fighters) / within 1" (Vehicles) of an enemy. Limited actions: Fight or Retreat. Seriously Injured/Damaged models cannot Engage or be Engaged. Engaging is optional at the start of an activation. |
| <span id="statuses-seriously-injured">**Seriously Injured**</span> (Fighters) / <span id="statuses-seriously-damaged">**Seriously Damaged**</span> (Vehicles) | Limited actions; must make a Recovery test after activating. |
| <span id="statuses-out-of-action-ooa">**Out of Action (OOA)**</span> | Roll on the D66 Lasting Injury (Fighters) or Lasting Damage (Vehicles) table. |

See also [Nerve Tests](#nerve-tests).
