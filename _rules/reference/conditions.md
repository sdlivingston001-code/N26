---
title: Conditions
order: 2
---

Minor, persistent effects a model can suffer in addition to its status.
{: .text-muted .text-small}

| Condition | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Injured** (Fighters) / **Damaged** (Vehicles) | Gained when Wounds reach 0. Can't use skills. |
| **Out of Ammo** | Can't shoot the affected weapon until it is reloaded. |
| **Webbed** | Can't move or be moved; -1 to hit rolls. Can break free at the end of an activation by passing a Strength check. |
| **Insanity** | Roll on the insanity table; pass a Willpower check at the end of an activation to recover. |
| **Intoxicated** | -1 WS and BS, +1 Cool. |
| **Blind** | Loses its ready marker, or the next one gained. Only hits on a natural 6 in close combat. |
| **Rad-poisoned** | -1 Toughness. |
| **Concussed** | -1 Initiative until the end of its next activation. |
| **Frenzy** | +1 attack whilst frenzied; must charge the closest enemy if possible. |
| **Hatred (X)** | Re-roll failed to-hit rolls in close combat against X. |
| **Fearsome** | Enemies must pass a Willpower check when they engage a Fearsome model, or fight at WS 6+. No check is required if the enemy is also Fearsome (only against Terrifying). |
| **Terrifying** | As Fearsome, and enemies must also pass a Willpower check to charge or engage this model, or lose their activation. No check if the enemy is also Terrifying. |
