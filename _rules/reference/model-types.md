## Fighter

Unless a model is designated as a **Vehicle**, it is a **Fighter**.

## Vehicle

Vehicles have restricted actions during battle, skill access, and Post-cycle actions.

Vehicles and their crew are inseparable and when the rules refer to Vehicles, they can refer to either the Vehicle itself or its crew.

When a Vehicle is removed from the Gang Roster, all weapons and wargear it is equipped with are deleted instead of going into the gang's Stash.

All Vehicles have the [Juggernaut](#skills-inherent-juggernaut) skill.
