title: Seriously Injured
order: 2
---

A Seriously Injured (Fighter) or Seriously Damaged (Vehicle) model's actions are limited to:

- **Crawl** (Double, Fighters) — move up to Strength".
<!-- - **Freewheel** (Double, Vehicles) — move up to D6". -->
- **Tend Wounds** (Double, Fighters) — roll an extra Injury die when making its Recovery test.
- **Conduct Repairs** (Double, Vehicles) — roll an extra Injury die when making its Recovery test.
- **Desperate Escape** (Double) — goes Out of Action. Roll a D6: on a 1 or 2, also roll on the Lasting Injury (D66, Fighters) or Lasting Damage (D66, Vehicles) table.

If you were already Seriously Injured at the start of your turn, you must make a Recovery test after activating — see [Recovery Tests](#recovery-tests).
