---
title: Nerve Checks
order: 11
---

If a friendly model (not a pet) goes Out of Action within 3", pass a **Cool** check or become **Suppressed**.

- [Prospects](#model-types-fighter-subtypes-prospect) only cause this check for other Prospects.
- Friendly models within 12" and in Line of Sight of a [Leader](#model-types-fighter-subtypes-leader), or within 6" and in Line of Sight of a [Champion](#model-types-fighter-subtypes-champion), may use that model's Cool characteristic for the check.
