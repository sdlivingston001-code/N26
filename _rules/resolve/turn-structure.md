title: Turn Structure
order: 1
---

Each battle round consists of three phases, resolved in order.

## <span id="turn-structure-priority-phase">1. Priority Phase</span>

**Ready Gangs**: Each player gives each of their models a ready marker.
**Roll for priority**: Each player rolls a D6, whoever rolls the highest has Priority for the round.
- If the roll is a draw, the player who did **not** have priority in the previous round wins.
- If the first roll for Priority is a draw then players re-roll until one player wins.

## <span id="turn-structure-action-phase">2. Action Phase</span>

**Activate Gangs**: Starting with the player who has priority, players take turns choosing one of their **Ready** models to activate.

## <span id="turn-structure-end-phase">3. End Phase</span>

<span id="turn-structure-end-phase-bottle-check">**Bottle check**</span>: If one or more models went Out of Action this round, starting with the player who has priority:
- Make a Leadership check against the highest Ld in the gang.
- If failed, remove one model from the battlefield (remove 2 models if the check was failed with a roll of 12). Only remove an Engaged model if there is no other option. Seriously Injured/Damaged models must check whether they succumb.

<span id="turn-structure-end-phase-voluntarily-flee">**Voluntarily Fleeing the battlefield**</span>: once a third of a gang's fighters are Out of Action, that player may choose to flee the battle.

<span id="turn-structure-end-phase-victory-check">**Check for Victory**</span>: once a third of a gang's fighters are Out of Action, that player may choose to flee the battle.

<span id="turn-structure-end-phase-reinforcements">**Reinforcements**</span>: resolve any reinforcement arrivals.
